- `D-UI-BUTTONS`: The route being drawn green is a judgement about pixels and stays with the operator. The transport recalculation is read from the log ('Transport mode changed to bike' then 'Starting route calculation with transport mode: bike'), not from the map.

- `D-SET-TUNE`: Save applying the correction to the receiver (FSD-SET-tune) is not checked - it would change the hunter's calibration.

| `bench` | yes | A Mac with Xcode. No hardware, no network, no sonde. | Always available; this is the pre-push gate. |

| `second-sonde` | yes | Two sondes in the picker, so a change has somewhere to go. | Payerne usually lists two. |
| `sonde-flying` | yes | A sonde airborne now. | Wait for a launch; Payerne flies to a schedule. |
| `sonde-descending` | no | A sonde after burst, falling. | Wait for the burst of a flight already being tracked. |
| `sonde-landed` | no | A sonde down and still lying out, its flight on SondeHub. | Wait for a flight to end within reach. |
| `sonde-refrequency` | no | A sonde whose reported frequency changes mid-flight. | Not arrangeable; watch for one. |
| `test-sonde` | no | A repowered bench sonde transmitting, absent from SondeHub. | Power the bench unit and tune the receiver to it. |
| `network-outage` | on-demand | Taking SondeHub away and giving it back. | Airplane mode on the phone - a person can do it, and a prompt can wait for them. |
| `hunter-driving` | on-demand | The hunter actually moving between towns. | A drive. Cannot be simulated: the route renewal rules key on real movement. |
| `hunter-on-foot` | on-demand | The hunter walking the last stretch to the sonde. | Being at the landing site. |

**Rig limitations, with their consequence**

- `simulator`: No CoreBluetooth: the simulator can never exercise the receiver, so every BLE clause needs the phone.
- `iphone`: XCUITest terminates the app under test at teardown; ui-cycle.sh relaunches it afterwards.
- `receiver-retune`: Using it during a live flight costs the campaign its BLE phases, so it is held back while a flight is in progress.
- `sonde-flying`: Opportunistic - a missed phase is missed for that flight.
- `sonde-descending`: The one stage that cannot be arranged at all.
